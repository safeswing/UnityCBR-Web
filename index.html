Description

Unity:CB Remaster is a fan redo of SCP: Containment Breach. It's made in Unity 6, built from scratch, and uses assets from the original CB, Ultimate Edition Reborn, and a few kind creators credited below.

Android v1.4 is planned for November 16–17, 2026. Release timing may change.

Story

You play as a Chaos Insurgency spy dressed up as a Level 4 researcher. Problem: you lost your keycard on the way in. Now you're hunting keycards around the facility like it's your first day again.

You came in through Gate B to grab intel, steal some classified docs, and get out. Halfway through, you find a room where the floor just ends. Nothing but void beyond it.

The floor ends. Beyond it is void. You step onto it anyway.

Who Are You?

You are a Chaos Insurgent Spy who has worked in the facility for months. Today is the final day. Your task is to retrieve classified documents, avoid hazards, gather intel, and return to C.I. You're disguised as a Level 4 Researcher. But something goes wrong…

Facility

What is a Flame Gate?

Flame Gates replace Tesla Gates and are riskier and costlier. it fires 2 flame fires from the top. This is no natural fire; it originates from SCP-███████ and deals roughly 1 billion damage per 0.25 tick, making it the ultimate weapon against non-immortal SCPs. It may not affect SCPs that are merely immortal.

Lore

Reveal story spoilers (And Endings!)

Midway through exploring, there is a void: no floor, just black space. You don't know it yet. You only have a Level 2 keycard, even though the rooms ahead require Level 3 or above. Why don't you have your Level 4 clearance? You are a Level 4… you just lost it.

You step inside the void and suddenly spawn back at the Elevator—the beginning, Gate B, where you started. You try to return to the surface, but the elevator has no buttons; it's completely gutted. The environment looks the same, yet darker, creepier, and distinctly wrong. You walk back to the exact spot where the void was… and the path continues. Download the game to experience more.

Note on Medical SCP-500

When you die in one of the Gate [REDACTED] endings, Nine-Tailed Fox (NTF) shoots you on sight. At first, failing to recognize a Senior Researcher is a major infraction, and the squad is demoted. However, the demotion is quickly revoked after a deeper investigation reveals you were actually a spy—earning the squad a reward instead. The entire time, you were wearing a bodycam recording your playthrough. This information is partially taken from Log 3.

In Log 4, MTF and a Hazmat team retrieve your bodycam and hand it over to higher-level clearance, such as the O5 Council. Meanwhile, a few Hazmat operatives are sent into the void to search for anomalies, where they discover a new variant of SCP-500 designated SCP-500-M (Medical). It has been taken in for further testing. It is currently unknown if it functions identically to the original SCP-500, or how many instances remain inside the Void Dimension.

Log 3 and Log 4 will be fully documented here soon. This covers one part of Ending 1 for Gate A.

Ending 1 Explained

After NTF eliminates you and recovers your bodycam, the subsequent investigation confirms you are a Chaos Insurgency (C.I.) spy. Reviewing the site's CCTV footage, personnel notice you vanished completely at 03:02:24 right after entering a specific room. Investigators also recover several stolen Foundation items and classified files on your person. However, because the originals are still accounted for and secure inside the facility, it is concluded that these duplicates were pulled from the Void Dimension.

Intelligence assumes G.P.A. and the Chaos Insurgency collaborated to engineer this "Void Matter" to sow chaos. While reviewing your bodycam footage, analysts observed trapped SCPs, deceased site personnel, and even fallen NTF operatives inside the anomaly. The Void Dimension appears to be a 1:1 mirror copy of Site-[REDACTED]. Response teams also discovered the bodies of armed Chaos Insurgents near Gate [REDACTED].

This incident occurred right after you took the elevator to the surface, somehow teleporting from the Void Dimension back into the real world. By then, the Gate [REDACTED] surface was an active warzone jets tearing overhead, helicopters idling, and personnel fleeing the compound. You were spotted roaming the rubble and fire on the ground when an NTF patrol intercepted and terminated you, leading to the events of this ending.

Research is currently underway to determine how the Void Dimension connected back to the real world via the elevator. This implies there are far more entry and exit points than just the initial Void Matter incident where the spy fell through.

Facility

SCP 966 Information and behaviors

SCP 966 is a persistent hunter, while it's Intelligence is Unknown, we have tested multiple times that it has roughly 20% chance to not avoid any threat Infront of it such as, Flame gates. However we noticed it is weak, thin bones, and overall just weak. We have estimate it has roughly 3000hp. Thus making it quite strong but not strongest, it can be killed. And we also noticed other behavior where it retreats if below 1000hp. Within 27 meter radius and 59% speed increase. It may be a survival instinct. It's Original Speed is 1.7, and casual being around 1.3 to 1.5.

Facility

SCP 173 Neutralization

SCP 173 has been neutralized by Chaos Insurgents due to a unknown failed incident occuring in their side. We have put our every effort to stop this but we couldn't stop. Scp 173 no longer exists, we will keep SCPs more securely and tight this time.

Facility

Orgins of GPA

GPA stands for Ground Protection Alert. It's an copycat of SCP foundation and an secret organization, so secret no one knows what they do. Even the foundation can't help here. What we know they are alleged to be working with chaos insurgency, G.O.C, and Making collaborative futuristic weapons. They remain highly classified and is more tighter than SCP Foundation, Black mesa.

Facility

Cause of Void and GPA Leaks

The void reality was secretly opened by GPA, with spies operating inside facility site-[redacted]. Their identities remain unknown, but it has been confirmed that GPA is behind the cause of the void. This information was leaked by the Chaos Insurgents following an argument several years ago. Relations appear to be healthy now, and the 05 Council is deciding whether to make a move against GPA.

Development

Pipeline Unity, and About Unity Versions

v1.4 and below
High Definition Render Pipeline (HDRP).
v1.5+
Universal Render Pipeline (URP).

Why Universal Pipeline in the newer version? Because the engine for now uses Unity 6.7 Beta, but in future we will switch to Unity 7, which adds CoreCLR and boosts performance. HDRP will be deprecated in the future, and URP will also receive more updates often and look like HDRP. So we converted while we can.
