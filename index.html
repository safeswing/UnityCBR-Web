Unity:CBR (Remaster)

A fan remaster of SCP: Containment Breach, rebuilt from scratch in Unity 6.

Description

Unity:CB Remaster is a fan redo of SCP: Containment Breach. It's made in Unity 6, built from scratch, and uses assets from the original CB, Ultimate Edition Reborn, and a few kind creators credited below.

Android is planned, but it won't arrive until the first stable version or later. Burnout is a real thing and I'd like to actually finish this one.

Follow or join the Discord Most update leaks and news end up there.

Story

You play as a Chaos Insurgency spy dressed up as a Level 4 researcher. Problem: you lost your keycard on the way in. Now you're hunting keycards around the facility like it's your first day again.

You came in through Gate B to grab intel, steal some classified docs, and get out. Halfway through, you find a room where the floor just ends. Nothing but void beyond it.

The floor ends. Beyond it is void. You step onto it anyway.

Android Project 2027

This is a complicated situation. After the Stable PC version is released, the game will be rebuilt for Android with a budget of 3 months.

Why 3 months? Why not more? Because of my personal life: I'm leaving my home country for the UAE, from September to late August. I do not have a laptop for portability, and everything is worked on PC.

These plans can change if I get a laptop.

Chance of getting a laptop
70%

However, if I don't get the laptop, 3 months may not be enough to rebuild it fully. In that case it can be released as a final beta version: rushed, and some features may be missing.

Simple terms Android will be rushed. Do not expect stable, or it may even be cancelled!
